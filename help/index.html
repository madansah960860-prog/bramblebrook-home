Orders

Do I need to create an account to buy something?

No, and there is no way to create one. Every order on this site is a guest order. You can browse, buy, read every policy, and start a return without a login or a password.

Can I order over the telephone instead?

Yes. Call (555) 010-0100 during Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern Time (closed weekends and federal holidays) and we will take the order down for you, including the card details, and read the total back before anything is charged.

How do I know my order went through?

A confirmation email arrives within a few minutes of payment, listing what you bought, the total charged, and what happens next. If it has not arrived within an hour, check the spam folder and then call us — it is usually a mistyped address, which we can correct.

Can I change or cancel an order after placing it?

Yes, free of charge, any time before it ships. Call (555) 010-0100 or email support@bramblebrookhome.com with the order number. Once a parcel has left us it becomes a return rather than a cancellation.

Shipping

How much is shipping and how long does it take?

Standard shipping is $7.95 and takes 3–7 business days. Expedited is $16.95 and takes 2–3 business days. Both estimates are counted from the day the parcel ships, not the day you order. Standard shipping is free on orders over $75.

How long before my order ships?

We pack orders in 1–2 business days. Orders placed at the weekend or on a federal holiday start counting on the next business day.

Where do you ship to?

The 48 contiguous states and Washington, D.C. PO boxes by Standard shipping only. No Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, US territories, or APO/FPO addresses yet.

What happens if my order is going to be late?

We write to you before the date we originally promised, give you a firm new shipping date, and offer you the choice of waiting or cancelling for a full refund. If we cannot give you a definite date, or the new date is more than 30 days out, the order is cancelled and refunded unless you tell us you would rather wait.

Can I track the parcel?

Yes. When the parcel leaves us you get an email with the tracking number and a link to USPS or UPS. Tracking usually starts updating within a day of the parcel being collected.

Returns

How long do I have to return something?

30 days from the delivery date. That is the delivery date, not the order date, so shipping time does not eat into it.

Can I return something just because I changed my mind?

Yes. An item does not have to be faulty. It needs to come back unused, in original packaging, with all parts and accessories. A prepaid return label costs $6.95, which we take off the refund, or you can post it back yourself at your own cost with any carrier you like.

What if it arrives damaged, faulty, or is the wrong item?

Then it costs you nothing. Tell us within 7 days of delivery, send a photograph, and we email a free prepaid label and send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

Is there anything you will not take back?

Slippers and robes that have been worn outdoors or washed cannot be returned, for hygiene reasons. Everything else in the shop can come back within the 30-day window. There is no restocking fee on anything.

When do I get my money back?

Refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving the return. Banks may take 3–10 more business days to post it.

Do you do exchanges?

No direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order. It sounds less convenient than it is: the refund and the new order run in parallel, so you are not waiting for one before the other.

Payment

What can I pay with?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Payment is handled by Stripe on their own secure page. We never see or store your card number.

When will my card be charged?

At the moment you confirm the payment on the Stripe page, and for the exact total shown there including tax. There are no later charges, no subscriptions, and no fees added afterwards.

Do you charge sales tax?

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address, where required. It is calculated and shown on the payment page before you confirm, so you always approve the precise final figure.

Products

Whose brand are these products?

Everything is sold under our own name, Bramblebrook Home. We do not resell other companies' branded goods, and nothing on this site is affiliated with or endorsed by any other retailer, organisation, or government body.

Are the photographs the actual items?

Not yet, and we would rather say so. The current images illustrate the product type while we photograph our own stock. Every measurement, weight, and material in the specifications describes what actually ships.

Will you tell me what something weighs before I buy it?

It is already on the page. Every item lists its dimensions in inches with centimetres in brackets, and its weight in pounds with kilograms in brackets, in the specifications table.

Accessibility

Can I make the text on this site bigger?

Yes. The A, A+, and A++ buttons at the top of every page step the whole site up from 18 to 21 to 24 pixels, and the choice is remembered on your next visit. Your browser's own zoom works as well, and the layout holds together at 200 per cent.

Can I use this site without a mouse?

Yes. Everything works from the keyboard, every focused element shows a thick outline, and a skip link at the very top jumps straight past the navigation to the page content.

What if I cannot use the website at all?

Call (555) 010-0100 during Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern Time (closed weekends and federal holidays) and we will place the order for you, read out any part of the site you want to hear, and email a copy of any policy page. If something on the site is getting in your way, tell us and we will fix it — see the accessibility statement for how we handle that.
