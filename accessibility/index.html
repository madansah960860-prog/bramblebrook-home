Our commitment

This shop is meant to be usable by everyone who wants to buy from it, including people using a screen reader, a keyboard alone, voice control, screen magnification, or a browser zoomed well past its default.

Accessibility here is not a plugin or an overlay widget. It is how the pages are built: real headings, real labels, real buttons, and text that stays legible when you make it larger. We have deliberately not installed an accessibility overlay, because overlays frequently interfere with the assistive technology people already have set up and working.

The standard we build to

We target WCAG 2.2 Level AA, published by the World Wide Web Consortium. Where we can reasonably exceed it, we do:

  • Body text contrast is 15:1 against the page background, against a AA requirement of 4.5:1.
  • Buttons and links are at least 48 by 48 pixels, against a AA requirement of 24 by 24.
  • The focus outline is 3 pixels thick, against the 2 pixel figure in the AAA focus-appearance criterion.
  • Body text starts at 18 pixels rather than the usual 16, with a line height of 1.65 and lines no longer than about 66 characters.

What is built in

  • Text size control. The A, A plus, and A double plus buttons at the top of every page step the entire site from 18 to 21 to 24 pixels. Your choice is remembered for your next visit. Everything scales together, so nothing overlaps.
  • Browser zoom to 200 per cent and beyond without losing content, and the layout reflows to a single column at 320 pixels wide rather than forcing sideways scrolling.
  • Full keyboard operation. Everything works with Tab and Enter, in a logical order, with a clearly visible focus outline. A "Skip to main content" link is the first thing you reach.
  • No sticky header. Nothing floats over the page, so the element you have tabbed to can never be hidden behind a bar.
  • Nothing moves on its own. No carousels, no autoplay, no timeouts, no infinite scroll, no pop-ups. Long lists use a "Show more" button you press when you are ready.
  • No dragging required for anything. Price filters are typed into number boxes rather than dragged on a slider.
  • Icons always carry visible words. No icon on this site is the only label for anything.
  • Status messages are announced. "Added to cart" and similar confirmations are read out by screen readers, not only shown.
  • Forms behave properly. Labels sit above the field and stay there, errors are described in words next to the field they belong to, and nothing you typed is ever cleared when a form fails to submit.
  • No CAPTCHA puzzles. We use an invisible technique to catch automated submissions instead, so you are never asked to identify traffic lights.
  • No account and no password is required to browse, buy, read a policy, or start a return.
  • Reduced motion respected. If your device asks for reduced motion, the few transitions on the site switch off.
  • Policy pages print cleanly if you prefer paper.

How we check

Every page is tested automatically with the axe accessibility engine on each build, and a build that produces a serious or critical issue does not ship. We also check every page by keyboard alone and at 400 per cent zoom, at widths from 360 to 1440 pixels.

Automated testing catches perhaps half of what matters. Manual checking finds the rest, and people telling us finds what we missed.

Known limitations

We would rather list these than pretend they are not there.

  • The payment page is not ours. Card payment is handled by Stripe on their own pages, and their accessibility is outside our control. If you have trouble there, call (555) 010-0100 and we will take the whole order over the phone instead.
  • Product images currently illustrate the product type rather than showing the exact unit. Their alt text describes what is shown accurately, and the written specifications are the authoritative description of what ships.
  • The filter and sort controls on the shop page need JavaScript. All the products themselves are in the page without it, and every product, policy, and help page is fully readable with JavaScript switched off.

If the site is in your way

You can place any order on this site by telephone instead. Call (555) 010-0100 during Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern Time (closed weekends and federal holidays). We will describe the items, read out prices, sizes, weights, and materials, take the order, and read the total back to you before anything is charged. There is no extra charge for ordering this way and no minimum.

We will also read out or email you any part of the site, including any policy page in full.

Telling us about a problem

If something here does not work for you, we want to know, and a rough description is plenty — "the buttons on the cart page are hard to hit on my phone" is genuinely useful.

Email support@bramblebrookhome.com, call (555) 010-0100, or use the contact form and choose "Accessibility of this website".

We reply within 1 business day with what we found and what we are going to do about it. Where a fix is quick we make it and tell you when it is live; where it is not, we tell you honestly how long it will take and how you can get what you needed in the meantime.
