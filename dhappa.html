How ordering works

  1. You place the order

    No account, no password, no sign-up. Add what you want to the cart and pay as a guest. If you would rather order by phone, call (555) 010-0100 during our hours and we will take it down for you.

  2. We pack it in 1–2 business days

    You get an email when the box leaves us, with the tracking number from USPS or UPS. If anything is going to be slower than that, we write to you before the date we promised and you can cancel for a full refund.

  3. It arrives in 3–7 business days

    That is Standard shipping, counted from the day it ships. If it turns out not to suit you, you have 30 days from the delivery date to send it back.
