Privacy policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

There are no accounts on this site, no analytics, and no newsletter. What we hold is what an order requires: who you are, where the parcel goes, and what you bought.

The one thing worth reading carefully is Your privacy choices, which covers advertising and how to opt out of it.

What we collect, and where it comes from

We collect as little as we can get away with while still sending you the right box. There is no account system on this site, so there is no profile accumulating behind the scenes.

Categories of personal information
CategoryExamplesWhere it comes from
IdentifiersName, delivery address, email address, phone numberYou, when you order or contact us
Commercial informationWhat you bought, order value, returns and refundsGenerated when you order
Payment informationCard type, last four digits, and whether payment succeededOur payment processor. We never receive or store your full card number
Internet activityIP address, browser and device type, pages visited, referring siteAutomatically, through our hosting provider's logs
Message contentAnything you write in the contact form, an email, or tell us on the phoneYou

We do not collect sensitive personal information as US state privacy laws define it: no government identifiers, no financial account logins, no precise geolocation, no biometric or health data, no racial, religious, or union information. We do not ask your age or date of birth.

Why we use it

  • To take payment, pack your order, and deliver it to you
  • To send transactional email: order confirmation, shipping notice, delay notice, and refund confirmation
  • To answer your questions and handle returns and warranty claims
  • To keep the required tax and sales records
  • To detect and prevent fraudulent orders and abuse of the site
  • To measure and target our advertising — see the advertising section below

We do not use your information to build a profile of you, we do not sell it for money, and we do not send marketing email. There is no newsletter to be added to.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that make the shop function, and only with what each one needs.

  • Our payment processor (Stripe) handles the card transaction on its own pages. It receives your name, email, billing and delivery address, and the order amount. We do not receive your card number at any point.
  • Our shipping carriers (USPS and UPS) receive your name, delivery address, and phone number so the parcel can be delivered.
  • Our hosting and email providers process the site and transmit our messages to you, and keep standard server logs.
  • Google, in connection with our advertising, as described in the next section.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it, to enforce our Terms of service, or in connection with a sale or reorganisation of the business — in which case this policy would continue to apply to information transferred.

Cookies and similar technologies

This site uses browser local storage, not cookies, for two things: remembering what is in your cart, and remembering your chosen text size. Both stay on your own device, neither is sent to us, and clearing your browser data removes them.

We do not run any analytics on this site. There is no Google Analytics, no heatmap tool, and no session recorder.

Our advertising does involve a third party — see below. Apart from that and the payment page, the site makes no requests to anyone else. Fonts, icons, and images are all served from our own domain.

Advertising

We advertise through Google Ads. That can involve Google receiving an identifier associated with your visit, so that it can show our advertisements to people who have looked at our products, and so we can tell whether an advertisement led to a sale.

Under California law this counts as "sharing" personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, even though no money changes hands for your data. Several other states describe the same activity as "targeted advertising". You can opt out of it, at no cost and with no effect on your ability to shop here — see Your privacy choices below.

Your rights over your information

California residents have these rights under the CCPA as amended by the CPRA, and residents of a growing number of other states — including Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Texas, Oregon, and Montana — have closely comparable ones under their own laws.

We extend all of the rights below to every customer in the United States, regardless of which state you live in and regardless of whether we meet the thresholds that make a given law apply to us. It is simpler for everyone, including us.

  • Know and access. Ask what we have collected about you, where it came from, why we collected it, and who we disclosed it to. Twice in any 12 months, free.
  • Delete. Ask us to delete what we hold, subject to records we are legally required to keep, such as tax records of a completed sale.
  • Correct. Ask us to fix inaccurate information.
  • Portability. Receive a copy in a portable, machine-readable format.
  • Opt out of sale, sharing, and targeted advertising. See the next section.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect any, so there is nothing to limit.
  • No discrimination. Exercising any of these rights will never change the price you pay, the service you get, or how we treat you.

Your privacy choices

There are two ways to opt out of our sharing information for targeted advertising. Both are free and take effect the same way.

1. Global Privacy Control

We honor the Global Privacy Control signal automatically. If your browser or a privacy extension sends GPC, we treat it as a valid opt-out request for that browser the moment we receive it. You do not have to tell us separately, fill in a form, or identify yourself. Browsers and extensions that support GPC include Firefox, Brave, DuckDuckGo, and Privacy Badger.

2. Ask us directly

Email privacy@bramblebrookhome.com with the subject "Opt out of sharing", or call (555) 010-0100 during Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern Time (closed weekends and federal holidays). We action it within 15 business days, and you do not need to give us a reason or prove who you are for an opt-out request.

An authorised agent may submit an opt-out on your behalf; we will ask for written permission signed by you.

How to make a request, and how we verify you

Email privacy@bramblebrookhome.com, call (555) 010-0100, or use the contact form and choose "Privacy request".

Verification. Because there are no accounts, we verify you by matching what you tell us against what we already hold: typically the email address used for an order, the delivery address, and roughly when the order was placed. We will never ask you for a government identity document, a photograph of yourself, or your Social Security number, and we will not create a new record about you in order to verify a request.

Timing. Opt-out requests within 15 business days. Access, deletion, and correction requests within 45 days; if a request is genuinely complicated we may extend that once by a further 45 days, and we will write and tell you why before we do.

How long we keep things

  • Order records: seven years, because tax and accounting rules require it.
  • Support messages: two years after the conversation ends.
  • Server logs: 90 days.
  • Opt-out records: kept indefinitely, because we need to remember that you opted out in order to keep honoring it.

Nothing is kept indefinitely except that last one. When a retention period ends, the record is deleted.

Security

The whole site is served over HTTPS. Card details are entered on our payment processor's own pages and never touch our systems. Access to order records is limited to the people who need it to pack orders and answer your questions.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we would rather say that plainly than imply a guarantee we cannot make. If a breach affects your information we will notify you as required by the law of your state.

Children

This site sells household goods to adults. It is not directed to children under 13, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and we do not ask anybody's age.

If we learn that we hold information from a child under 13, we delete it. A parent or guardian who believes we hold such information should contact privacy@bramblebrookhome.com and we will remove it promptly.

We do not sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16 for advertising purposes.

Changes to this policy, and contacting us

If we change this policy we update the "last updated" date at the top of the page. Where a change materially affects your rights we will say so prominently rather than quietly editing the text.
