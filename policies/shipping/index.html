Shipping policy

Last updated September 21, 2026

What it costs, how long it takes, and what we do if something goes wrong. Every figure on this page is the same figure you will see in your cart, at checkout, and on the product pages.

Where we ship

The 48 contiguous states and Washington, D.C. PO boxes by Standard shipping only. No Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, US territories, or APO/FPO addresses yet.

PO boxes are fine by Standard shipping, which travels with USPS. Expedited shipping cannot go to a PO box because the carrier requires a street address for that service.

We do not ship outside the United States. If you place an order with an address we cannot deliver to, we will contact you and refund it in full rather than letting it sit.

How long before your order ships

We pack and hand over orders in 1–2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding federal holidays. An order placed on a Saturday starts counting on the following Monday.

Processing time is separate from delivery time. The delivery estimates in the table below begin on the day the parcel leaves us, not the day you place the order.

A small number of oversized items ship by Standard only. Where that applies it is stated on the product page and in your cart, and the Expedited option is switched off at checkout.

Carriers and tracking

We ship with USPS and UPS. Which one carries a given parcel depends on its size and the destination; we do not let you choose the carrier, only the speed.

When the parcel leaves us you get an email with the tracking number and a link. Tracking normally begins updating within one business day of collection. If your tracking has not moved in three business days, contact us and we will open an enquiry with the carrier.

If your order is delayed

We only advertise shipping times we can actually meet. If something changes and we cannot ship within the time stated when you ordered, here is exactly what happens, and it follows the Federal Trade Commission's Mail, Internet, or Telephone Order Merchandise Rule.

  • We write to you before the original promised date — not afterwards.
  • We give you a firm new shipping date, or tell you honestly that we do not have one.
  • We offer you the choice to cancel for a full refund, and you can say so by replying to that email, by calling (555) 010-0100, or through the contact form. It never costs you anything to respond.
  • If the new date is 30 days or less beyond the original one and we hear nothing back from you, we take that as agreement to wait.
  • If the delay is longer than 30 days or we cannot give a date, we cancel and refund the order unless you tell us you would rather wait.
  • If a second delay follows the first, silence is never agreement. We need your explicit yes, or we refund you.

Refunds for a cancelled order are issued within one billing cycle for a card payment. You get the full amount you paid, including the shipping you paid.

Lost, stolen, and damaged parcels

Marked delivered but not there: check with anyone else at the address and look around the property first, then contact us. Parcels occasionally get scanned early. If it has not appeared within two business days of the delivery scan, we file a claim with the carrier and send a replacement or refund you — we do not make you wait for the claim to settle.

Tracking stops moving: if a parcel has not moved in seven business days we treat it as lost and send a replacement or a full refund, whichever you prefer.

Arrives damaged: tell us within 7 days of delivery and send a photograph of the item and the outer box. We email a free prepaid return label and send a replacement or refund at no cost to you. Full detail is in the Returns and refunds policy.

Risk of loss passes to you on delivery, but as the paragraphs above make clear, we deal with the carrier rather than sending you to do it.

Wrong or incomplete addresses

Check the address on your confirmation email as soon as it arrives. If something is wrong, call (555) 010-0100 or email support@bramblebrookhome.com immediately — before the parcel ships we can change it for free.

If a parcel is returned to us because the address was incomplete or incorrect, we refund the cost of the items in full. The original shipping charge is not refunded in that case, because the carrier has already been paid for the journey. We will happily send the order again to a corrected address at the normal shipping rate.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on the shipping address, where required.

The tax amount is calculated and displayed on the secure payment page before you confirm, so the figure you approve is the figure charged to your card. Tax is not added after the fact.
