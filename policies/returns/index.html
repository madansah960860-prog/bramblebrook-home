How long you have

You have 30 days from the delivery date to start a return. We use the delivery date rather than the order date deliberately, so time spent in transit does not come out of your window.

"Start a return" means contacting us within those 30 days. The parcel itself does not have to be back with us by then — once we have issued your label you have a further 14 days to post it.

Changing your mind is a valid reason

An item does not have to be faulty for you to send it back. If the colour is not what you pictured, the throw is heavier than you wanted, or it simply does not suit the chair, that is enough. You do not have to explain yourself, and we will not ask you to justify it.

Condition: Unused, in original packaging, with all parts and accessories. We understand you have to open the box and look at the thing to know whether you want it — that is not "used". What we cannot resell is an item that has been slept under for a fortnight, laundered, or is missing its parts.

Exactly what a return costs you

If you changed your mind: $6.95. We email a prepaid return label and deduct its cost from the refund. Customers may ship with their own carrier at their own cost instead. If you would rather use your own carrier, you may — you pay that cost directly and we deduct nothing from the refund. We recommend a tracked service, because until it reaches us we cannot refund it.

If the item is damaged, defective, or is not what you ordered: nothing at all. Free prepaid return label or a free replacement, if reported within 7 days of delivery with photos. We also refund the original shipping you paid in these cases, which we do not do for a change-of-mind return.

Restocking fee: None. We do not charge one on anything, in any circumstances.

How to start a return, step by step

There is no account to log into, no returns portal, and no form number to quote. Pick whichever of these suits you.

  1. Get in touch by phone on (555) 010-0100 during Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5 p.m. Eastern Time (closed weekends and federal holidays), by email to support@bramblebrookhome.com, or through the contact form.
  2. Tell us what is coming back. The order number helps but is not required — your name and the delivery address are enough for us to find it. Say whether you changed your mind or something is wrong with the item.
  3. We email you a prepaid label the same business day, or the next one if you contact us outside our hours. It is a PDF you print. If you cannot print, say so and we will post a printed label to you at no charge.
  4. Pack it up in the original packaging with all the parts and accessories, fix the label to the outside, and hand it to the carrier or drop it at any of their locations.
  5. We refund you within 5 business days of the parcel arriving with us, and email you when we do.

How and when you get your money

Refunded to the original payment method within 5 business days of receiving the return. Banks may take 3–10 more business days to post it.

Refunds always go back to the card or wallet you paid with. We cannot send a refund to a different card, and we do not offer store credit in place of a refund unless you specifically ask for it.

What you get back: the full price of the returned items, plus tax on those items. For a change-of-mind return, the original shipping charge is not refunded and the $6.95 label cost is deducted. For a damaged, defective, or wrong item, you get everything back including the original shipping, and nothing is deducted.

If your refund has not arrived: allow the 3 to 10 business days your bank may take after we issue it. If it has been longer than that, contact us with the order number and we will send you the refund confirmation from our payment processor, which your bank can trace.

Reporting damage or a fault

Tell us within 7 days of delivery and include a photograph — of the item, and of the outer box if the box is damaged too. A phone photograph is perfectly adequate; we are not assessing your photography.

We will not ask you to send the item back before we put things right. In most cases the replacement goes out the same day we see the photograph.

Faults that appear later are covered by the warranty: If an item has a manufacturing defect within 90 days of delivery, we replace it or refund it in full, including original shipping.

What cannot come back

This is a short list, and each entry has a reason we can actually state.

  • Slippers and robes that have been worn outdoors or washed. For hygiene reasons we cannot resell these, so they cannot be returned once worn outdoors or laundered. Trying them on indoors is fine and does not affect your right to return them.

Everything else in the shop can be returned within the 30-day window. Where an item has a restriction, it is also stated on that item's own page before you buy it.

Exchanges and cancellations

Exchanges: No direct exchanges. Return the item for a refund and place a new order. In practice this is usually faster than a true exchange, because the new order ships immediately rather than waiting for the old item to come back.

Cancellations: Free any time before the order ships. Contact support by phone or email. Call (555) 010-0100 or email support@bramblebrookhome.com with the order number. A cancellation before shipment costs nothing at all and is refunded in full, including shipping.

Questions about this page
