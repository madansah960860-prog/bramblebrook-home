About Bramblebrook Home

Bramblebrook Home sells soft goods and small comforts for the rooms people actually spend their evenings in. Throws and blankets, house slippers and robes, seat and back cushions, reading wedges and lap desks, floor matting, kitchen cloth, and flameless light. There are 21 items in the shop, arranged by room rather than by product type, because most people arrive thinking about a particular chair rather than about a textile category.

The shop is aimed at adults furnishing a home they have lived in for a while and want to be comfortable in. That shapes the buying more than anything else: we are interested in things that are light enough to pick up, simple enough to operate without a manual, and labelled in type you can read across a room.

How we choose what to sell

Four questions decide whether something goes in the shop.

What does it weigh, and can you lift it one-handed? A throw that takes two hands to shake out gets left on the sofa. We publish the weight of every soft item in pounds, and where a heavier version and a lighter version both make sense we stock both and say plainly which is which.

Can you grip it, open it, and work it? That means zip pulls long enough to hook a finger through rather than small metal tabs, battery doors that twist open without a coin or a fingernail, switches you can find by feel, and buckles you press rather than thread. Where a product has a control, we describe the control.

Can you read it? Where something has a display or a setting, we say how big the numbers are and whether they stay lit. A three-quarter-inch digit that stays on is worth more than a dozen features you cannot see from a chair.

Does it hold up? Foam that flattens in a month, a slipper back that folds down under the heel, a pocket that gives out at the bottom fold — these are the failures that make a product not worth the money. Where we know the specific part that usually fails, we say what has been done about it.

We do not make health claims about any of it. These are household goods described by their measurements and materials, and that is all we will ever say about them.

Ordering, shipping, and returns

There is no account and no password. You can browse, buy, read every policy, and start a return without creating one, and you can order by telephone during our hours if you would rather not type a card number into a website. Payment on the site is handled by Stripe on their own secure page; we never see or store your card number.

We pack orders in 1–2 business days and ship by USPS and UPS. Standard shipping is $7.95 and takes 3–7 business days from the day it ships, free on orders over $75. If an order is going to be later than we told you, we write to you before the date we promised, offer you a firm new date, and let you cancel for a full refund instead. That is not a courtesy, it is how mail-order selling is required to work in the United States, and we would rather state it than bury it.

You have 30 days from the delivery date to send something back, and changing your mind counts — an item does not have to be faulty. A prepaid return label costs $6.95 and comes off the refund, or you can post it yourself at your own cost. If something arrives damaged, defective, or is simply not what you ordered, the label is free and so is the replacement.
