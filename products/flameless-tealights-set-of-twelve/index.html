What it is

Twelve tealights, each one and a half inches across and three quarters of an inch tall, which is the standard size, so they drop into tealight holders you already own. The switch on the base is a raised rocker rather than a flush slider — you can find it and flip it by feel, without turning the light over to look. Each one also carries its own six-hour daily timer, so a whole windowsill of them can be set once and left. The flame is a warm amber, steady with a slow drift rather than a fast flicker, which reads better in a group than twelve lights blinking out of step. Batteries are included and installed, and each light takes one CR2032 that changes by twisting the base a quarter turn. Expect roughly 120 hours from a set of batteries at the daily timer setting.
