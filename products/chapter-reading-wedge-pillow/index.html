What it is

A wedge does what a stack of pillows keeps failing to do, which is stay in one shape for an hour. This one is 24 inches wide, 21 inches deep, and rises to 12 inches at the back on a steady slope, which sits most people at a comfortable reading angle without pushing the head forward. The core is two layers: a firm base that carries the shape and a one inch layer of softer foam on top so the surface is not board-like. It weighs 4.6 pounds, light enough to carry from the bedroom to a chair in one hand by the sewn-in side handle. The cover is a fine knit that unzips along the full back edge, and it is worth noting that the zipper pull is an inch-long fabric loop rather than a small metal tab. There are no straps and nothing to assemble.
